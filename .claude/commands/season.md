description: The season on one screen - kilograms in, trays packed, in store and dispatched per crop and variety, and the money per pool. The owner's morning glance.
---

1. Run `npm run packhouse -- season`. Two tables: the fruit (per crop and variety) and the money (per pool).
2. `stats` is the same story in five lines, with the attention count on the end.
3. Read it as movement, not totals: what came in this week, what is stuck in store, what dispatched and whether its proceeds have landed (`unsold` on a pool is money missing).
4. The printable pages are `npm run view`: the packhouse board (decisions, intake, consignments, coolstore) and the registers (stock, pools, returns, GAP), in the business's brand.
