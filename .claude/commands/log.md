---
description: A call, a decision, a fact from the floor - onto the record the moment it happens, against a delivery, a pallet, a consignment or a grower.
---

1. Run `npm run packhouse -- log <DEL-ref | PLT-no | CON-ref | grower> "what was said or decided"`.
2. Log at the moment, not at the end of the day: the residue result, the buyer's answer, the reason a hold was released, the promise a grower made. The season's memory is this table.
3. If the target is ambiguous the CLI lists candidates rather than guessing; pick one and run it again.
4. The log shows up on the matching card (`delivery <ref>`, `consignment <ref>`, `grower <name>`), which is where it earns its keep.
